An IoT-based hydroponics monitoring and automation system with integrated AI crop disease detection, purpose-built for urban farms.

Raspberry Pi 5 / YOLOv8n / Built for urban farms

AI-Drophonics combines a Raspberry Pi 5 edge device, real-time YOLOv8n object detection, and automated sensor-driven responses to help urban farmers catch crop disease before it spreads, without watching over their farm all day. Built by SOURCE for Gulayan at Bulaklakan Integrated National Urban Farm, Quezon City.

Your farm, in focus

The AI-Drophonics companion app gives urban farmers real-time visibility into pH, temperature, humidity, and water levels, live disease detection alerts via push notification, AI-generated treatment recommendations, and a centralized dashboard, all from an Android device.
